Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

At EarthNsol, accessible from earthnsol.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by EarthNsol and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

If you register for an account, buy products, or interact with our site, we may collect information including, but not limited to:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, phone number, and physical address for shipping and billing.
  • Transaction Data: Details about payments and purchases you make on our store. (Please note: We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers; payment processing is handled securely by our payment gateway integrations).
  • Session Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited, collected via cookies and site logging to enhance user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and e-commerce services.
  • Process and deliver your orders, and notify you of order statuses.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website to optimize page layouts and product offerings.
  • Communicate with you for customer service, updates, and marketing communications (where permitted).
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and protect our customers from security breaches.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information. When you submit sensitive information via the website, your information is protected both online and offline. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized employees and service providers who need it to fulfill orders or provide customer support.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, EarthNsol uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information with our business partners and trusted affiliates for the purposes outlined above, or with delivery partners (e.g., courier services) to fulfill shipping.

6. Your Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

7. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at earthnsol@gmail.com.
